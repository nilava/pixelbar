Pairing

Changing anything needs a token. Press this, read the six digits off the panel, and type them in \u2014 the token is then kept in this browser. Reading the panel's state never needs one.

Touch pads

Buttons drive the pads as levels, not as events, so the gesture recogniser sees exactly what a finger produces. Hold latches — press it again to let go, and hold two at once for a chord.

Brightness

Floors at 24: below that a growing share of the panel sits at a PWM value of one to three, where these LEDs are least well behaved.

Firmware

Pick firmware/build/pixelbar.bin. The panel shows a bar while it writes and restarts itself when it is done. Do not unplug it during the write — that is the one thing that can actually brick the device. If the new build fails to run for ten seconds the bootloader puts the old one back on its own.

Mirror

What the panel is showing, about ten times a second. These are the framebuffer's own colours, before gamma and the current cap — what the screen means, rather than what the LEDs receive.
